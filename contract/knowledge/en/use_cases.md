# Use cases by persona

## Agencies and their clients

- Before launch the client opens the site, clicks the elements and writes or
  says what to change. The agency gets each comment with its place and a saved
  view instead of screenshots in a spreadsheet or a long e-mail.
- The team clarifies in the thread at that spot and resolves it when done.
  External reviewers need no account, on every plan and without a limit.
- After the fixes, a review round lets the team check each change on the live
  page and mark what still needs work.
- Guest links for agency clients and white-label client workspaces are not
  available.

## Founders and product teams

- Users leave feedback exactly where they got stuck (a sign-up step, a form,
  a button) with the page and the element attached.
- On a landing page, people from the audience say what is unclear in the
  offer. Treat it as a lead to check, not a diagnosis.

## In-house teams

- Everyone comments on the page itself instead of a shared document, and each
  comment keeps its element, so nobody has to guess what it was about.
- On Team and Public addresses the team decides which threads a coding agent
  may work on by handing them over. See `address_modes`.

## Developers with coding agents

- The agent reads comments over MCP with the element's selectors and the whole
  thread, team notes included, and locates the element in the code.
- It plans fixes with the human, ships them and, with the owner's permission,
  resolves the discussions, replies in the thread or prepares a review round.
- On request it creates a project or adds an exact address the human names,
  installs the widget and confirms it once the site is opened in a browser.
