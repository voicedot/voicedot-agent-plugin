# How Voicedot works

1. **Install once.** The Voicedot snippet (a configuration line and one
   script) goes into the site's shared layout. A project collects comments
   only from its exact HTTPS addresses (scheme, host and port), local
   development included.
2. **Point and comment.** A visitor clicks an element, then types what to
   change, or says it where the plan includes voice. The text can be
   corrected before sending. No account, no extension, no screenshots.
3. **Context travels with it.** Each comment stores the address, the page
   path, the selected element and a saved view of the page. Values typed into
   forms are masked in that view.
4. **Discuss and decide.** In the inbox the team sees the comment, its place
   and the conversation. A reply in a pin thread appears to the commenter in
   the widget on that page, in the same browser, while the pin is open; it is
   not e-mailed. A reply to a whole conversation is e-mailed when the visitor
   left an e-mail address; otherwise it is kept as an internal note.
5. **Stay informed.** When a review session has been quiet for 15 minutes,
   Voicedot e-mails one digest of its new comments to the owner; other
   recipients depend on the plan. AI sorts comments into categories.
6. **Close the loop.** Resolve a discussion when it is done. Closing a pin
   hides it from the live page while the discussion stays in the inbox. A
   conversation can be exported as Markdown, or a coding agent can work from
   it over MCP.
7. **Check the fixes.** After fixes ship, a review round lists each change
   with what to check; the team marks every item Checked or Needs fix in the
   Voicedot app, on a phone too. See `review_rounds`.

The knowledge base lets the team save answers once and generate a suggested
reply on demand while answering a conversation.
