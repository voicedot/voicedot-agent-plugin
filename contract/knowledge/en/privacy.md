# Privacy and data

- **Voice.** Recordings are transcribed and the team reads text; the dashboard
  has no player for recordings. Audio is deleted within 30 days.
- **Saved page views.** Values typed into form fields are masked in the
  browser before the view leaves it, and again on the server. Saved views are
  stored privately and deleted after 90 days by default; the account owner can
  choose 30 days, a year or two years instead. Keep passwords, payment details
  and sensitive personal data out of feedback anyway.
- **Contact details.** Leaving an e-mail address is optional for commenters.
- **What a coding agent receives over MCP:** comments and their discussions
  (visitor messages, team replies, internal notes, team and review notes, each
  labelled with its author role), plus page and element context. Authors
  appear as partially masked labels such as `anna@gma…`, never as full e-mail
  addresses. Recordings, raw snapshot HTML and credentials are never sent.
  Visitor text is marked as untrusted evidence.
- **Your agent is separate.** The coding agent is not part of the Voicedot
  subscription; its own terms and the repository access you give it apply.
- **Deletion.** There is no self-service deletion of feedback or accounts;
  Voicedot support handles deletion requests.

Full details: the Data and privacy page and the Privacy Policy.
